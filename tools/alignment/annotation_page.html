Maldon LIDAR help needed

The route from Witham to South Woodham Ferrers has been traced from 1 m LIDAR. Most of it is solid. These are the places where your eyes beat my data. Maldon Reborn

How this works

  1. Pick a view below. The grey-pink picture is the LIDAR terrain at 1 m per pixel; green is my traced railway; pink dots are stations; cyan squares are bridges and tunnels from the county survey (good to about 70 m).
  2. Tap to place a marker (choose what it is first) or switch to Draw line and tap along the real railway where my trace is wrong.
  3. Press Copy results and paste them into the Claude chat. Every tap is converted to National Grid coordinates for me.

Drag to pan, pinch or use +/− to zoom. Your marks are saved in this browser, so you can come back to it.
