Maldon East and Maldon West

Ground: Environment Agency 1 m LIDAR (2022), so the 1990 bypass and later building are in it. "As built" shows the stations new (1848 and 1889); the other button shows how they stood in the early 1980s. Layout: the 1897 Ordnance Survey plans reproduced in the Essex County Council survey (2011), placed using today's roads and the Lovers Lane crossing (about ±25 m). Buildings, heights, the overbridge, the rolling stock and the Maldon West cutting are inferred and drawn as simple blocks.
