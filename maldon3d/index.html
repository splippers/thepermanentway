Maldon East and Maldon West, 1980s

Ground: Environment Agency 1 m LIDAR (2022), so the 1990 bypass and later building are in it. Layout: the 1897 Ordnance Survey plans reproduced in the Essex County Council survey (2011), placed using today's roads and the Lovers Lane crossing (about ±25 m). Buildings, heights, the overbridge and the Maldon West cutting depth are inferred and drawn as simple blocks.
