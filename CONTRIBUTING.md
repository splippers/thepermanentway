# Contributing material

The Permanent Way runs on research, not invention. Each corridor (see
`docs/ROADMAP.md`) needs station-level detail to the standard of Mission 1:
photographs, plans, memories, corrections. If you hold material for a lost
or surviving line — your own photos, a society archive, family memories,
a corrected date — this is how to get it in.

## What's useful

- **Photographs** — stations, bridges, goods sheds, platforms, track in situ
  or during demolition/lifting. Any era. Scans of prints are fine.
- **Plans and drawings** — OS extracts, engineering drawings, signal
  diagrams, estate plans showing the line.
- **Corrections** — a wrong date, a misattributed structure, a demolition
  year we've marked uncertain. These are often more valuable than new photos.
- **Memories and local knowledge** — what a stop was actually like, names
  that don't appear on any plan, why a bridge was really closed.
- **Sources we should cite** — journal articles, society newsletters, books,
  even if you can't share the content itself, the pointer is useful.

## What we need alongside it

For each item (or each batch from one source), fill in the submission
template at `docs/contributing/SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md`. The short version:

1. **Who took/made it, and when** (as best known).
2. **Who you are**, and whether you're the rights holder, submitting with
   the rights holder's permission, or it's your own work.
3. **What we're allowed to do with it** — research reference only, or
   reproducible with credit, or public domain/your own CC-licensed work.
4. **Credit line** — how you want to be named, or "no public credit."

We will not knowingly republish copyrighted photographs without the
rights holder's permission, regardless of how they reach us. If you're
sending someone else's scan (an archive, a society collection), please
check with them first, or just tell us who to ask and we'll do it.

## How to send it

- **Preferred:** open a pull request adding your files under
  `refmaterial/<corridor>/<your name or source>/`, with the filled-in
  template alongside.
- **No GitHub account / can't use git:** email jon@splippers.com, or ask
  about the project's Samba/file-drop point if you've been given access to
  it. Include the same information as the template either way.
- **Just a correction, no file:** open a GitHub Issue describing it, with
  a source if you have one.

## What happens next

Material lands in `refmaterial/<corridor>/` for research — it isn't
automatically used in the game. It gets folded into a corridor's structure
list, mission text, or the 3D viewer once someone's checked it against
other sources (the project cross-checks rather than relies on a single
photo — see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). You'll be credited per your stated
preference wherever it's used.

If the Society or group you belong to would rather we not reference you
publicly, say so — we'll keep the material for research only and leave
your name out of it.
